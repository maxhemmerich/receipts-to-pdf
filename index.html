    What is in the PDF

    Cover index
    Every receipt listed in date order with its amount, then a total for the whole batch.
    One receipt per page
    The photo is fitted to the page and centred. Nothing is cropped or cut off.
    Footer on each page
    Which receipt it is (3 of 7), its date and its amount, so pages survive being printed and shuffled.
    Size you can email
    Photos are resized to 2200 pixels on the long edge, so a stack of receipts stays a few megabytes.

    Your photos stay on your machine

    There is no upload. There is no server that could receive your files. The page ships a content-security policy that blocks every network request, so it cannot send your pictures anywhere even if a bug tried to. The PDF is assembled by the JavaScript already running in this tab.

    You can check all of that: the full source is on GitHub, and the same policy line is visible in the page source.

    Receipts carry your card number, your address and what you buy. That is why this one does not have a server.
